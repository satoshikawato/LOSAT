- New program `losat makeprofiledb` (NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 makeprofiledb with `-dbtype rps`): builds the profile databases that `losat rpsblast` and `losat rpstblastn` search, such as CDD, from a list of PSSM files in ASN.1 text (CDD's `.smp` files with scores, psiblast's `-out_pssm` files, and files with frequency ratios only, whose scores LOSAT computes as NCBI does). Every file of the database is NCBI's byte for byte: the `.rps`, `.loo`, `.aux` and `.freq` files and the protein BLAST database of the profiles' sequences (version 5, or 4 with `-blastdb_version 4`), several volumes with `-max_smp_vol` and their alias file, with NCBI's messages, exit statuses and the files left after an error. Queries taken from a BLAST database keep their binary definition lines (ids, taxonomy and titles), as NCBI's do. Every option of `makeprofiledb -help` for `-dbtype rps` runs (`-threshold`, `-force`, `-scale`, `-matrix`, `-gapopen`, `-gapextend`, `-index`, `-taxid`, `-taxid_map`, `-title`, `-out`, `-logfile`). Not yet: `-binary` (profiles in ASN.1 binary) and `-dbtype delta` or `cobalt`, which are refused. Inputs on which NCBI's makeprofiledb crashes, aborts, does not end or reads outside its data are refused with a message (`-max_smp_vol 0`, `-in` naming a directory, frequency ratios too large for its integers, profiles stored by row with more than 28 positions, a query without Seq-ids or descriptors), and so are damaged binary definition lines. A word score threshold or profile scores near the limits of 32-bit integers, where NCBI's sums overflow, are compared with their exact values.
