- `losat rpsbproc` writes the sites and motifs of protein queries, `-t feats` and `-t both` (NCBI's default), as NCBI's rpsbproc 0.5.1 writes them, byte for byte: the header lines of `SITES` and `MOTIFS`, `SITES` (the specific sites of each representative curated hit, the generic sites of its hierarchy root from `cdtrack.txt`, the sites of structural domains with source `0`; a site counts when more than 80 % of its positions map to the query) and `MOTIFS` (structural motifs, those of structural domains cut to the query ranges the model keeps), with NCBI's PROSITE-pattern parser and matcher for the motif check of a site (its errors, exit 3 for a motif that does not parse, and its process-wide list of the first motif site's positions, kept per run), the one-letter residues of NCBIeaa, IUPACaa and NCBIstdaa queries, and `-t feats` printing no `DOMAINS` or `SUPERFAMILIES`. Refused as before: rpstblastn's archives (nucleotide queries) and the XML input; refused with the sites: a motif match that reads the site's residues beyond their end, and a site or motif outside the query's residues (NCBI reads memory outside the string). A motif check that reads NCBI's motif position list past its end counts as failed, since NCBI's result depends on heap bytes (PD-LOSAT-NCBI-DEFECTS 1.13; line PS, series PR, item PS16b; DECISIONS 24, 25 and 27).
