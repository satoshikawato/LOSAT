- The 8-bit AVX2 X-drop DP (the gapped alignment of `blastn` and the score-only gapped alignment of `blastp`, `blastx` and `tblastn`) masks the lanes past the end of the matrix only in the one block of a row that reaches it, instead of testing and masking in every block (`LOSAT_X_DPEDGE`, one of the switches above since 2026-11-24; `=0` or `LOSAT_X_DEFAULT=0` turns it off). Every lane holds the same value as before, so reports do not change. On the workstation `blastn` (31,168 Drosophila cDNAs against a 3 Mb genome, one thread) takes 0.972 of the time and `blastp` (WSSV against PajaWSV) 0.989; max RSS is the same ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
