- `blastn -db NAME -db_hard_mask ID` searches with the database's masks of the filtering algorithm `ID` cut out of the search, with the output of NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 in every format: the database sequences are searched in pieces between their masks, an alignment that crosses a mask in the traceback scores the masked letters as `B`, and the identities of the displayed rows (outfmt 0, 5, 13 to 16, tabular rows with sequence columns) count the stored letters while the Seq-align's (outfmt 8, 9, 11, 12, the standard tabular columns, SAM) count the masked ones as mismatches. A database with a searched sequence whose only mask runs to its end (all masked, or a masked tail), whose masks are out of order, or whose last mask is shorter than the letters before its end in its last piece is refused before anything is written, where NCBI BLAST+ reads past its buffers (line NP, M4a-2b).
