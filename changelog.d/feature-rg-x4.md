- `blastp -use_sw_tback` runs NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0's Smith-Waterman redo with composition-based statistics (`-comp_based_stats` 1, 2 and 3): each match's alignments are found with the composition-adjusted matrix by a full Smith-Waterman scan, NCBI's start search and its X-drop traceback, in NCBI's order and with its significance and hit-list tests, on the kernels of `rpsblast -use_sw_tback`. Still refused: `-use_sw_tback` with `-comp_based_stats 0`, in `blastx`, `tblastn` and `psiblast`, a match whose redo never ends in NCBI, a query batch whose redo fails where NCBI's traceback fails, and with `-db` and `-num_threads` above 1 a match whose query already has `-max_target_seqs` redone matches (NCBI's result then depends on how its threads split the matches).
