- `blastn` with `-db` at two threads or more makes about one traceback scratch per worker instead of one per parallel task: a sequence takes a scratch another one gave back (or makes one when none is idle). Outputs do not change. 100 16S rRNA queries against the 16S database with `-task megablast` at 16 threads made 22,032 scratches of about 100 KiB in the traceback; now at most one per thread and batch.
