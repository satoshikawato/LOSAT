- `losat blastn` takes `-best_hit_overhang` and `-best_hit_score_edge`, as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does, with `-subject` and `-db`: of the alignments of a query, those that a better one covers are removed (the Best Hit algorithm), both where NCBI filters the preliminary results (with the score edge only) and after the final alignments (with both values). Either option turns the filter on, and the one not given is 0.1; values must lie above 0 and below 0.5, and neither works with `-culling_limit`. The BLAST archive (`-outfmt 11`) records the options given. LOSAT Web shows them as "Best hit overhang" and "Best hit score edge" for BLASTN, and `pylosat.Search` takes `best_hit_overhang` and `best_hit_score_edge` for it. BLASTP, BLASTX, TBLASTN, TBLASTX and PSIBLAST still refuse the two options.
