- `tblastx` at one thread runs the unit path of `LOSAT_X_PAIRPAR` for query batches of more than about 2 kb (a reference diagonal table of 2^13 cells or more) instead of 65 kb: the units are now the faster path there (`LOSAT_X_PAIRPARN1`, a new switch of the strict set; `=0` restores the 65 kb line; same results). 5 kb of a virus genome x the E. coli genome takes 0.88 of the time (0.444 -> 0.391 s), 10 fruit fly transcripts x the zebrafish cDNA database 0.91 (45.86 -> 41.56 s); in WebAssembly 0.69-0.79. Queries of 2 kb or less and searches on several threads keep their path.
