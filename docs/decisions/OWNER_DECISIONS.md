# Owner decisions cited by the repository

The repository cites the Owner's decisions in two ways: by date ("Owner, 2026-10-17", "on the
Owner's request (2026-11-04)", "accepted by the maintainer on 2026-10-02", "保守者の指示（2026-10-04）",
a row of the Web plan's DW log) and by an entry of a work line's decision log ("drop-in survey
`DECISIONS.md` 12", "v4-C DECISIONS 25"). The logs are kept outside the repository. This file
copies each cited decision: its date, the Owner's words as written (in Japanese) with a summary in
English, and where the repository cites it. A few entries that the repository does not cite are kept
because a cited entry depends on them ("related entry").

As of dev 6ecdb2bf2 (2026-11-06).

- Kind: Owner (the Owner's own words, or the Owner's answer to a question), Owner, in part (an entry
  that records both), Owner-delegated (a working session decided it under the Owner's standing
  delegation of 2026-09-29, restated 2026-10-07; the entry has no words of the Owner), Record (an
  entry that records a fact, not a decision), Not found (the repository cites the Owner and a date,
  but no words of the Owner for it were found in the work records; the row gives what they, or the
  repository, say).
- An Owner-delegated decision is not the Owner's approval where a rule asks for one (Owner,
  2026-11-05); [`SWITCH_APPROVALS.md`](SWITCH_APPROVALS.md) applies this to the stop rule for
  conditional switches.
- Cited in (first section): the number of citing lines found, and the first two. A line counts when
  it names the Owner (the maintainer, 保守者) near the date, in any wording, also across a line break,
  or is a DW row of the Web plan that records the Owner's instruction or answer.
  A line that cites an Owner-delegated decision or the standing delegation does not count. Inside
  `docs/decisions/`, only lines that quote the Owner's words count. Lines that give only the date of
  a decision whose entry is in the second section (for example line GZ entry 8, "the Owner's ask of
  2026-11-04") are counted in neither section.
- Line and stage IDs are in [`LINE_IDS.md`](LINE_IDS.md).
- When the repository cites a new decision, add its row here in the same pull request.
