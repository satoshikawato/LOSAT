- `losat psiblast` starts from a multiple sequence alignment. `-in_msa FILE` (`-in_msa -` reads the standard input) makes the query's PSSM from a Clustal alignment and then searches it as `-in_pssm` does: the rounds (`-num_iterations`), the PSSM files (`-out_pssm` and `-out_ascii_pssm` get the input PSSM first), `-export_search_strategy`, and the reports of outfmt 0, 6 and 7. `-msa_master_idx N` takes the N-th sequence as the query instead of the first, and `-ignore_msa_master` builds the PSSM without counting the query sequence. The PSSM, its files and the search are NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0's, as are the errors for a file that cannot be read, a master index past the rows, a master that matches no row, and a matrix or gap costs that its score block does not take (PS6; DECISIONS 74 of line PS). An alignment in FASTA, PHYLIP, NEXUS, Sequin or MultAlin format is refused by name, and so are the inputs on which NCBI crashes or writes past its buffers: a master sequence of X, O, `*` or lower-case x alone, a master that is the start of a longer sequence above it (put it above that sequence), and `-matrix IDENTITY`. `-phi_pattern` stays refused. The library and pylosat refuse the three options: make the PSSM with `-out_pssm` and read it there.
