- `blastp -db` reads the subject of each match from the database when the composition redo reaches it, as NCBI does, instead of a copy of every subject of the preliminary hit lists made before the redo; the reports read again the subjects they show (`LOSAT_X_BPREDODBSUBJ`, one of the switches of the strict set, so reports and errors do not change; `LOSAT_X_BPREDODBSUBJ=0` turns it off; `LOSAT_X_BPREDODBSUBJSHADOW=1` also makes the copy and compares every read with it) ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
