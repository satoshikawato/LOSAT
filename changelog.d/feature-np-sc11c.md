- TBLASTN in composition mode 0 (`-comp_based_stats 0`, and every IDENTITY search, which NCBI also turns to mode 0) translates its traceback windows from a table of codon residues instead of codon by codon (`translate_frame_into`), and after a fence reads windows of the whole translation of a frame around each HSP, widened when the traceback reaches their end (`x_whole_window`), where it translated whole frames of the subject for every list traced again: a chromosome's frame is millions of residues. The traceback of the lists of a search runs on its threads, as NCBI's does. The output is unchanged.
