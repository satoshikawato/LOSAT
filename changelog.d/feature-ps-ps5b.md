- `losat psiblast -in_pssm FILE` runs its rounds and writes its PSSM files as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does, byte for byte, with `-subject` or `-db` and outfmt 0, 6 and 7: with `-num_iterations N` (0: until convergence) round 1 searches the input PSSM and each later round the PSSM that NCBI's PSSM engine makes from the round before, whose query keeps the input PSSM's Seq-id (a `local id` stays an integer) and title (`title ""` without one), with NCBI's composition-based reset warning once (round 1). `-out_pssm` and `-out_ascii_pssm` (with `-save_each_pssm` and `-save_pssm_after_last_round`) get the input PSSM at the start of round 1, also with one round, as NCBI's object writes it: a PSSM of frequency ratios only with the scores and gapped values its PSSM engine computes (and its ratios padded to 28 rows), the ASCII PSSM in NCBI's short form (`Last position-specific scoring matrix computed`, the scores and the PSSM's PSI Karlin-Altschul values) or, for a PSSM that holds its information content, the long one; after a round 1 without hits `-save_pssm_after_last_round` writes the input PSSM again with the Karlin-Altschul values of its search (NCBI's write-back). Refused before the search: with `-out_pssm`, a PSSM with members that LOSAT's reader skips and NCBI writes back, and a PSSM of frequency ratios only without `rpsdbparams` (NCBI stops with a C++ exception); with `-out_ascii_pssm`, a PSSM whose information content comes without the other columns NCBI's long form reads. A PSSM whose identifier or matrix name has a byte outside the visible ASCII range is refused as its titles are (NCBI prints an error for each such byte), and an empty `descr` gets NCBI's "Unrecognized format for PSSM" (line PS, item PS5b; DECISIONS 15).
