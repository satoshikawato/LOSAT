- `blastn -db NAME -db_hard_mask ID` searches a database sequence whose first mask runs to its end (all masked, or a masked tail such as a poly-A tail) with megablast, blastn and dc-megablast (a word of 11 letters or more): the sequence is searched before its mask, as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 searches its other pieces, which gives NCBI's output (apart from the version line, which names LOSAT) wherever NCBI's scan of the masked letters past that piece finds nothing more (an approved NCBI defect, PD-LOSAT-NCBI-DEFECTS exception 13; NCBI can crash there). `-task blastn-short` still refuses such a database (line NP, M4a-2c).
