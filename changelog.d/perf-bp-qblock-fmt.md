- `blastp` searches its queries in blocks of whole query batches and writes each block's report before it searches the next block for outfmt 0 (the default) and outfmt 7 too, not only for outfmt 6 (`LOSAT_X_BPQBLOCKFMT`, one of the switches of the strict set, so reports do not change; `LOSAT_X_BPQBLOCKFMT=0` turns it off; `LOSAT_X_BPQBLOCKFMTSHADOW=1` also searches all queries at once and compares). On the workstation 3,000 proteins against a proteome took 4.43 s with outfmt 0 at 16 threads (6.55 s before), 7.93 s at 8 (10.02 s) and 59.5 s at 1 (73.1 s); the yeast proteome against its own `-db` took 24.6 s at 16 threads (34.4 s), 40.1 s at 8 (56.6 s) and 281 s at 1 (354 s); outfmt 7 likewise; peak memory 36-58 MiB instead of 217-1,266 MiB; outfmt 6 unchanged ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
