- `blastn -task blastn` of a large query file against `-subject` at 12 to 64 threads runs at most eight `LOSAT_X_QBATCHPAR` slots: where the slot model would take more, its best layout of at most eight slots runs instead (`LOSAT_X_BNEIGHTSLOTS`, one of the switches above; `=0` keeps the model's choice): on 31,168 D. melanogaster cDNAs against 3 Mb at 16 threads, max RSS 702 -> 400 MiB (below NCBI's 630 MiB) in x0.98 of the time; at 32 threads, 1,440 -> 512 MiB in x0.93; on 4,000 cDNAs at 16 threads, 452 -> 214 MiB in x0.76. Outputs do not change.
