- `blastn -task dc-megablast` finds its seeds faster on large query sets: the scan of a word's query offsets reads the context index's form, the word length and the subject's bound once per strip or word instead of once per offset, and writes each word's seeds into spare list capacity when room fits the run (31,168 dmel cDNAs against 3 Mb of the dmel genome: 9.750 to 9.213 s at 1 thread, x0.945; 1.732 to 1.693 s at 8 threads, x0.977; 1.050 to 1.022 s at 16 threads, x0.973) (`LOSAT_X_DCEMITHOIST`, a switch of the strict set, so reports do not change; `=0` turns it off) ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
