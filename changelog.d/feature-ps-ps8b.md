- `losat psiblast -phi_pattern FILE` runs the rounds after PHI-BLAST's first (`-num_iterations` 0 or more than 1): the PSSM made from the alignments of the pattern's first place in the query, the later rounds, their convergence, the PSSM files (`-out_pssm`, `-out_ascii_pssm`, `-save_each_pssm`, `-save_pssm_after_last_round`) and the search strategy are NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0's. PHI-BLAST's report is also written as `-outfmt` 1 to 4 (with the `pattern` line), 5 (one `Iteration` for each place of the pattern), 11 (the first round's report, then the archive of the later rounds), 18 and with `-html`, and a database sequence with the query's Seq-id under `-parse_deflines` shows the query in the tables, as NCBI's does. The library takes the pattern as `Inputs::phi_pattern` (pylosat: `phi_pattern=`), and its rows carry the place of the pattern (`occurrence`). Refused by name: two or more queries with rounds after the first (NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 crashes at the second query) and outfmt 0 of a database sequence with the query's Seq-id under `-parse_deflines` (NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 aborts). With a matrix and gap costs that PHI-BLAST has no statistics for, BLAST XML (`-outfmt 5`) gives NCBI's message, no hits and NCBI's statistics of -1 in every round, and the organism report (`-outfmt 18`), at which NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 stops with a null pointer exception, is refused there. `-taxids`, `-negative_taxids`, `-taxidlist`, `-negative_taxidlist` and `-seqidlist` post their database warnings once more for the rounds after the first, as NCBI does.
