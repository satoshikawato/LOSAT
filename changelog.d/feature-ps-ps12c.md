- `losat rpstblastn` takes `-use_sw_tback` with composition-based statistics (`-comp_based_stats` 1, the default, 2 and 3): each profile's matches are aligned again with NCBI's Smith-Waterman redo in the windows of each query frame, so the hits, scores, e-values and reports are those of NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 `rpstblastn -use_sw_tback`, byte for byte at every thread count, with `-strand plus`, `-searchsp` and `-seg`. Before, LOSAT refused it. A profile whose Smith-Waterman redo NCBI repeats without end is refused: the e-value of a score of 0 passes `-evalue` (a very large `-evalue`, a very small `-searchsp`, or `-strand minus`, with which NCBI's redo reads a search space of 0), a window of a query frame has several of the profile's hits and the profile's first position scores the window's first letter above 0. A profile whose Smith-Waterman alignment starts with a gap is refused for now: NCBI's reports cannot take the range of such an alignment, so NCBI warns and stops outfmt 0 to 5 and 13 to 16 there, and LOSAT does not write those reports yet (line PS, series PR, item PS12c; DECISIONS 38; PD-LOSAT-NCBI-DEFECTS 1.80).
