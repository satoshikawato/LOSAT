- `losat psiblast -in_pssm FILE` runs with the composition-based statistics of a PSSM as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does, byte for byte, with `-subject` or `-db` and outfmt 0, 6 and 7: `-comp_based_stats 1`, and the default 2 and 3 (and `T`, `D`), which NCBI runs as 1 with a PSSM and with its warning ("Composition-based score adjustment conditioned on sequence properties and unconditional composition-based score adjustment is not supported with PSSMs, ..."; the preliminary e-value cutoff is then not stretched), redo each subject's alignments on the PSSM rescaled for the subject's composition (NCBI's position-based `Blast_CompositionBasedStats`: the start matrix from the PSSM's frequency ratios with a factor of 1, the lambda ratio of the subject's composition, X, U and O as NCBI sets them, SEG on the subject unless its first alignment is near identical), scaled by 32, with the e-values and bit scores of the PSSM's gapped Lambda and the K of the factor-1 PSSM (settled with a probe of NCBI's library) and `Method: Composition-based stats.`; the footer keeps the PSSM's values. A PSSM whose frequency ratios are all zero (or absent) gets NCBI's warning and BLOSUM62's ratios in the redo; a PSSM of frequency ratios only gets its scores and gapped values from NCBI's PSSM engine (the ungapped values from the query). The redo runs the subjects in the preliminary hit list's order at every thread count (NCBI's one-thread order). Refused: a PSSM of frequency ratios only written by row (NCBI reads the scores it computes for it in the wrong order) and a PSSM whose position-based scaling gives no Karlin-Altschul parameters (line PS, item PS4b; DECISIONS 11 and 13).
