- makeblastdb: a nucleotide database builds faster from a genome with runs of N, and from most sequences with other IUPAC
  letters or with gaps; the databases do not change. The Drosophila melanogaster genome (BDGP6, 144 Mbp) takes 0.70 of
  the time at one thread (421-430 ms to 294-298 ms), with the same memory.
