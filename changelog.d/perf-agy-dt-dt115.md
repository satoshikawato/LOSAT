- blastdbcmd, blastdbcheck, and BLAST-database input to dustmasker, segmasker, and windowmasker take less time with the same output: each sequence's definition lines are now decoded into the buffers kept from the previous sequence (lines, titles, Seq-id lists, and Seq-id strings) instead of allocating and freeing a new set per sequence, and title visible-character replacement and counting run in a single 16-byte pass. On 300,000 human proteins, blastdbcmd `-outfmt "%a_%l_%T"` takes 12–14 % less time and FASTA output 6 % less; on 150,000 human cDNAs, `-outfmt "%a_%l_%T"` takes 9 % less; on NCBI Swiss-Prot and PDBaa, `-outfmt "%a_%l_%T"` takes 9 % and 14 % less time and FASTA output 4 % less.
