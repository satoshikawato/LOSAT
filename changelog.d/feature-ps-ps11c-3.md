- `losat rpsblast` takes `-use_sw_tback`: with composition-based statistics (`-comp_based_stats` 1, the default, 2 and 3) each profile is redone with NCBI's Smith-Waterman redo (`Blast_RedoOneMatchSmithWaterman`: the composition-adjusted profile, then locally optimal alignments one after another, each found by a score-only scan that avoids the earlier alignments' ranges, its start by a reverse scan and its traceback by an X-drop alignment, while the next one's E-value on the query's search space passes `-evalue`), as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does, byte for byte, at every thread count; without composition-based statistics NCBI's search does not read it, and neither does LOSAT's. The kernels read the score rows through the caller (a profile's rows now, a square matrix later). A profile whose redo makes an alignment of score 0 below the E-value threshold after an earlier one (an `-evalue` above the query's search space times K), which NCBI makes again and again until its memory runs out, is refused. `blastp`'s and `psiblast`'s `-use_sw_tback` stay refused (line PS, series PR, item PS11c-3; DECISIONS 26 D1 and D7).
