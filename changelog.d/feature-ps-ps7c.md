- `losat psiblast -in_pssm` and `losat tblastn -in_pssm` search a PSSM whose Karlin-Altschul values are negative, infinite (`PLUS-INFINITY`, `MINUS-INFINITY`, the NR text `INF`) or not a number (`NOT-A-NUMBER`), in ASN.1 text and binary, as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does, byte for byte (apart from the version line), where LOSAT refused them: the gapped X-drops of a block that NCBI holds invalid are 0, a word X-drop of 0 takes the cutoff, E-values that NCBI cannot make print `0.0` and bit scores `inf` or `-1.0`, and the report footer leaves out a block whose lambda is not 0 or more. A PSSM query whose Seq-inst `length` is one less than its letters is searched as its first `length` letters, as NCBI does; the other lengths stay refused (NCBI crashes or writes past its buffer) (line PS, item PS7c).
