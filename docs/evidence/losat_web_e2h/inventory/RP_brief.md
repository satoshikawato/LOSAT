# Range RP: reports that use what the reader produced (IDs and titles)

Files: `RP.tsv`, `RP_notes.md`. Read `COMMON.md` first.

The reader gives each record a local ID (`lcl|Query_N`, `lcl|Subject_N`) and a title (the defline bytes after `>` and the skipped white space, cut at the first byte below 0x20, trailing white space trimmed; see range RD). LOSAT today shows `bio`'s ID (first Unicode-white-space word) and description, and rejects every defline where the two differ. After the port, titles may be empty, contain any byte ≥ 0x20 including non-ASCII UTF-8 and non-UTF-8 bytes, and be longer than 1000 bytes. Trace every place the four programs print an ID or title, in outfmt 0, 6 and 7, and in warnings and errors, and say exactly what bytes NCBI prints for these titles.
