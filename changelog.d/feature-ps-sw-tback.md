- `psiblast -use_sw_tback` now runs, where LOSAT refused the option before: each match is aligned again with NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0's Smith-Waterman traceback, in the first round and in the PSSM rounds (`-in_pssm`, `-in_msa` and the later rounds of a FASTA query), with `-comp_based_stats` 0 to 3, with `-sum_stats true`, with `-db` or `-subject`, at every `-num_threads` as NCBI's `-num_threads 1` does it. The reports, the PSSM files (`-out_pssm`, `-out_ascii_pssm`, `-save_each_pssm`) and the PSSMs of the later rounds are NCBI's. A query or round whose last Smith-Waterman alignment fails ends as NCBI's does when the query has kept no alignment: the results of the queries and rounds before, then `LOSAT engine error: Traceback failed`. Still refused, after the results of the queries and rounds before: a query and subject whose alignment NCBI never finishes, and a query or round on which NCBI crashes, one whose last alignment fails after the query has kept one (the manual's page "What LOSAT refuses"). With `blastp -use_sw_tback -sum_stats true`, the refusal of an alignment that NCBI never finishes now says that a score of 0 reaches the cutoff score, where it named the score's e-value.
