- `losat blastn -ungapped` searches without gapped extension, as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does, in all four tasks, with `-subject` and `-db`: the HSPs are the ungapped extensions of the initial hits, re-evaluated for ambiguity codes in the preliminary stage (so `-perc_identity` and `-qcov_hsp_perc` act there), with the ungapped Karlin-Altschul block only (no Gapped block and no `Gap Penalties` line in outfmt 0, no gap costs in XML2 and JSON), and the query is not split. Reward and penalty pairs without a gapped table, and gap costs the gapped tables do not have, are accepted. Output format 17 (SAM) with `-ungapped` is refused until its form is written (formats 8, 9, 11 and 12 are written; see the line of the Dense-diag writer). Searches without `-ungapped` give the same output.
