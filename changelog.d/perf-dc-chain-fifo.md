- `-task dc-megablast` is faster with queries of up to a few hundred kilobases (whose lookup table keeps NCBI's chains): a subject word that may have hits is looked up a few words later, after its table cell was prefetched, instead of at once, so the scan no longer waits on memory for each such word (a zebrafish genome of 1.37 Gbp searched with 100 short cDNAs on one thread: about 12 % faster, and 8 % with `-db_soft_mask`). Reports do not change.
