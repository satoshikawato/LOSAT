- `-outfmt 17` (SAM) in `blastn` (all tasks), with `-subject` and `-db`, and its words `SQ` (the target's letters in SEQ) and `SR` (the subject as the reference): the document that NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 writes, byte for byte at every thread count except the `CL:` tag of its `@PG` line, which holds LOSAT's own command line (no `CL:` tag without one). NCBI keeps every record until its formatter is destroyed and writes the header last; LOSAT holds the records as rendered text until the search ends (less memory than NCBI's per-record strings) and, like NCBI, writes the earlier batches' document before the error of a failed batch. A database sequence's letters for `SQ` are read by range. Outfmt 0/5/6/7/8/9/12/13-16 are unchanged; the other programs keep NCBI's "SAM format is only applicable to blastn".
