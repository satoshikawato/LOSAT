- `blastn` holds less memory with many queries: a query's hit list no longer reserves room for 100 subjects when its first subject comes, and grows as subjects come (blastn-short of 30,000 reads against 3 Mb: peak anonymous memory 186 -> 99 MiB at one thread, max RSS 305 -> 199 MiB at 16 threads and 437 -> 334 MiB at 32, below NCBI's 389 MiB; 16 threads x0.96 of the time) (`LOSAT_X_BNHLGROW`, a switch of the strict set, so reports do not change; `=0` turns it off) ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
