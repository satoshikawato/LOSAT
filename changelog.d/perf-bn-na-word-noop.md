- BLASTN: `-task blastn-short`, and any blastn search whose word size is its lookup table's word, is 7-12 % faster at one thread with the same memory: the scan no longer asks for each seed's word type, which for such a table is always the seed itself. On the workstation, 24-base queries took 25.6 s instead of 27.6 s and 18-30-base queries 28.2 s instead of 32.0 s at one thread; 16 threads and megablast take the same time. Reports do not change.
