- `losat psiblast -phi_pattern FILE` runs PHI-BLAST, a search that keeps only the alignments containing a PROSITE pattern found in the query, for one round (`-num_iterations 1`) with `-db` or `-subject`: the pattern's places in the query, their E-values from NCBI's pattern statistics, the reports of `-outfmt 0` (a table and the alignments for each place, with a `pattern` line), `6`, `7`, `10` and `20`, the queries' messages, a query record without letters searched with the next query, a query of `X` alone, a query longer than blastp splits (searched whole, never in chunks), the search strategy file (`-export_search_strategy`) of several queries, the pattern errors, the error of `-outfmt 8`, `9` and `12` where an alignment is found, and the report of a matrix and gap costs without NCBI's pattern statistics (its message and no hits) are NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0's. Refused by name, after NCBI's own errors: rounds after the first, `-outfmt` 1-5, 11, 13-16 and 18, `phi_pattern` in the library and pylosat, and the patterns and hit counts that take NCBI past its arrays.
- `losat psiblast -phi_pattern` also runs with `-sum_stats true` (each subject's alignments linked into sets, with the sets' E-values and `sum_n`) and with subjects longer than 5,000,000 residues (searched in NCBI's overlapping parts, a pattern place in an overlap counted in each part; an alignment that the merge of the parts carries past the end of the query is shown with gaps there, as NCBI shows it), byte for byte as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0's reports at every thread count. Refused: such an alignment whose pattern region runs more than two letters past the end of the query or more than one past the end of the subject, where NCBI reads past its copy of the sequence.
