Even recovering 0.65 s from the measured 5.579 s suffices for the AP 5% target. LC cannot reach 5% from this component alone; it must pass nonregression and remains an unmet primary goal. ## Next hypotheses (not implemented or accepted)

The independent benchmark-boundary audit found the runner/filesystem confound in all five historical rejected experiments, with an additional Wasm filesystem mismatch. This is new causal evidence permitting a fresh investigation; it does not itself validate any old candidate. Preserve their old decisions and do not adjust old sample values using the new probe.

For BLASTN, the LC quiet instrumented run spends 0.160 s in traceback batches in a roughly 0.84 s cold invocation; preliminary DP adds about 0.12 s. A 5% cold improvement needs about 0.04 s. Pairing the fixed DP band and traceback band as mutable slices would remove per-cell index checks and pointer reconstruction while retaining the exact ascending NCBI loop, changing X-drop cursor independently of the captured band bounds. A roughly 15% reduction across those loops could meet that budget, but this is a hypothesis, not a measured attribution. The prospective candidate must start from B1, be independently tested against the same raw outputs, and pass controls before combination with C-N1. NCBI owners: `blast_gapalign.c` ALIGN_EX 511–519/563–635 and packed nucleotide preliminary DP 3145 onward. Batch size may also be reconsidered after the new filesystem evidence, but unchanged scheduling overhead must first be separated from actual alignment work.

For BLASTP, current instrumented n8 traceback calls visit 2.095/3.241 billion cells and 28.953/44.372 million rows on the two main inputs. Native/Wasm raw outputs agree, and current PC sampling ranks ALIGN_EX highest among mapped kernels. The existing trace helper updates Vec length and checks length/capacity on every main-band cell. Writing an already-reserved contiguous band through `spare_capacity_mut`, then exposing exactly the initialized prefix once per row, could remove that repeated bookkeeping without zero-filling cells or changing any recurrence/tie decision. The budget is at least 0.37/0.55 s from the observed roughly 7.31/10.93 s cold invocations. Aggregate instrumented worker CPU-like durations are not wall fractions and do not prove this budget. The fence path must expose only cells actually written, including zero; existing main-band and band-growth order and final used-row span must be retained. The current row allocator may not guarantee a requested capacity after reuse because reserve is relative to length, so an explicit per-row reserve sufficient for the actual band is required before any unsafe length update. This remains a proposed independent candidate, pending BLASTN disposition.

## C-N3 disposition and stable HSP storage

C-N3 exploration suggested LC6.20%/AP3.44% improvement. Five-pair confirmation instead gave LC-3.29%/AP5.09%; the required additional five LC pairs gave4.26%, below5%. Record INCONCLUSIVE and freeze without adoption or combination. All raw outputs and tested RSS/nonregression checks passed. Do not select only the favorable cohort or replace the target.

The user asked about NCBI repeatedly accessing stable data while LOSAT moves whole objects. C-N1 only bulk-moves full HSPs; it does not remove their31GB logical shift volume. C-N4 will therefore start from B1 and retain a fixed HSP arena during endpoint pruning, moving one-word nullable index handles during both stable sorts and shifts. The AP measured276,319,304 moves would transfer about1.1GB of4-byte handles instead of30.95GB of112-byte HSP slots; this is a structural byte-volume estimate, not a speed result. LC has only78,088 endpoint shifts, so no5% LC improvement is predicted from shift bytes alone. Sorting also stops moving full payloads, but its total contribution is not yet established. C-N4 addresses the verified data-layout cause and may replace C-N1 as the AP component. One O(N) arena and handle array avoid per-HSPBox allocations; materialization still moves survivors once at return. Delete payloads immediately, trim in place, retain stable incoming ties, same active/tail boundary, NULL-last order, and exact trace/re-evaluation timing. The independent preimplementation audit supports this design; native/Wasm raw/time/RSS gates remain required.
