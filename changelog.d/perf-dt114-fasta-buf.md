- blastdbcmd FASTA output takes less time with the same output and memory: each record is written into one buffer kept from one sequence to the next, with room for its letters and line feeds taken at once instead of grown line by line, and a title made of several lines is joined without a buffer per line. On 300,000 human proteins, FASTA output takes 9-10 % less time and `-entry_batch` of 30,000 accessions 7 % less; on 150,000 human cDNAs, FASTA output takes 5 % less.
