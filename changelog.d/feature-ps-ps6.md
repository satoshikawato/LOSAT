- `losat psiblast` takes `-in_msa`, `-msa_master_idx` and `-ignore_msa_master`, as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does (PS6; DECISIONS 74 of line PS). `-in_msa FILE` (`-in_msa -` is the standard input) makes the query's PSSM from a multiple sequence alignment in Clustal format and then runs as `-in_pssm` runs: the rounds (`-num_iterations`), the PSSM files (the input PSSM is written before round 1), `-export_search_strategy` with `-db`, the warnings and the reports of outfmt 0, 6 and 7. The format of the file is guessed as NCBI's reader guesses it; the Clustal scanner, the checks of the rows and of their Seq-ids, the choice of the molecule type, the master sequence (the first row, or `-msa_master_idx N`, swapped to the first row when an earlier row starts with its letters) and the PSSM engine's two options for alignments (the unaligned positions are ignored, and `-ignore_msa_master` leaves the master out of the counts) follow NCBI's code, so the PSSM, its files and the search are NCBI's. A file that NCBI cannot read, a master index past the rows, a master that matches no row, a query of no letters, a matrix or gap costs that NCBI's score block does not take, and a master with `*` (NCBI then ends with an exception after the prolog) end as NCBI ends them, with its message and exit status. An alignment in FASTA, PHYLIP, NEXUS, Sequin or MultAlin format is refused by name before anything is written (the Clustal format is the only one read), and so are the two inputs on which NCBI crashes: a master sequence of X or O alone, and `-matrix IDENTITY`. `-phi_pattern` stays refused. `losat-lib` and pylosat refuse the three options (the query of the library API is its records or a PSSM). The search strategy of a run with `-ignore_msa_master` records `IgnoreMsaMaster` as NCBI does.
