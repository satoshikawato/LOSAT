- `losat rpsblast` and `losat rpstblastn` write `-outfmt 11` (the BLAST archive, ASN.1 text, that `losat rpsbproc` and NCBI's rpsbproc read) and `-outfmt 5` (BLAST XML) with the bytes of NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 `-num_threads 1`, at every thread count and `-mt_mode`, for every option these programs run. Before, both refused these formats. `losat rpsblast -query proteins.faa -db Cdd -outfmt 11 | losat rpsbproc -d data -t doms -m rep` now runs as one pipeline with NCBI's archive bytes in between (one archive per query batch), for protein and nucleotide queries. The archive and the XML come from the writers of `blastp` and `blastx`: the request's service and options, the XML's program and version, the footer's K of the batch's first query and, for `rpstblastn` with composition-based statistics, NCBI's counts of identities and positives in the Seq-aligns are data of the RPS runs, with no second writer. Tests: frozen rows at outfmt 11 and 5 for both programs and a pipeline test of the archive and of rpsbproc's output against NCBI's.
