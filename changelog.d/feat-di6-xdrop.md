- `losat blastn` takes `-xdrop_ungap`, `-xdrop_gap`, `-xdrop_gap_final`, `-window_size`, `-off_diagonal_range` and `-no_greedy` as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does, in all four tasks, with `-subject` and `-db` (pyani's ANIb call, `-task blastn -xdrop_gap_final 150`, runs): the X-drops are bits that the search converts to raw scores once per query batch, the final X-drop is at least the preliminary one, `-window_size` gives megablast and blastn the two-hit word finder (0 gives dc-megablast one hit), `-off_diagonal_range` needs a window, and `-no_greedy` extends megablast by dynamic programming (refused with its default gap costs 0/0, as NCBI does). The archive (outfmt 11) records the X-drops and the window. A search without these options is unchanged. `-min_raw_gapped_score` stays unsupported.
