- `losat tblastx -strand plus|minus|both` searches the query's frames of the strand it names, as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does, with `-subject` and `-db` and in every output format: the frames of the other strand take no place in the query (cutoffs, search spaces, masks and frame boundaries are those of the searched frames), the average query length of sum-statistics linking still counts the six contexts of each query, outfmt 11, 13 and 14 keep NCBI's search space of context 0 (0 with `minus` unless `-searchsp` is given), and the subject is searched in all six frames. `-strand` is no longer refused; `both` (the default) gives the same output as before. LOSAT Web shows the option under "Query strand".
