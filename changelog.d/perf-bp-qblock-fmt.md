- `blastp` searches its queries in blocks of whole query batches and writes each block's report before it searches the next block for outfmt 0 (the default) and outfmt 7 too, not only for outfmt 6 (`LOSAT_X_BPQBLOCKFMT`, one of the switches of the strict set, so reports do not change; `LOSAT_X_BPQBLOCKFMT=0` turns it off; `LOSAT_X_BPQBLOCKFMTSHADOW=1` also searches all queries at once and compares).
