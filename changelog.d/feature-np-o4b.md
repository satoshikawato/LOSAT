- `losat blastp -ungapped -comp_based_stats 0` searches without gapped extension, as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does, with `-subject` and `-db`, in every output format that `blastp` writes (`-outfmt 8`, `9`, `11` and `12` with the alignment record NCBI writes for an ungapped HSP): the HSPs are the ungapped extensions of the initial hits, with each query's own ungapped Karlin-Altschul block (no Gapped block and no `Gap Penalties` line in outfmt 0, no gap costs in XML2 and JSON), and the query is not split. Gap costs outside the matrix's table, and `-matrix` names NCBI does not have built in, are accepted as NCBI accepts them (an unknown name gets NCBI's error). Refused by name: `-ungapped` with `-use_sw_tback` (NCBI crashes), with a `-matrix` name that begins with `IDENTITY` but is not `IDENTITY` (NCBI aborts), with a name NCBI reads from a matrix file, and with a subject of more than 5,000,000 residues. `-subject_besthit` works with `-ungapped` as NCBI's does (the step sees the ungapped HSPs before those of a negative length are dropped). Searches without `-ungapped` give the same output.
