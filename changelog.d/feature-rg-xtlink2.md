- `losat tblastx -window_size 0` with `-negative_seqidlist`: a subject whose only alignments are one-hit alignments of length 0 or less now has its definition lines read where NCBI BLAST+ reads them, so a database whose header there cannot be read is refused at that subject, as NCBI's search ends there (LOSAT used to go on).
- `losat tblastn -ungapped -window_size 0`: a subject of fewer than 12 bases whose only alignments are one-hit alignments of length 0 or less is no longer refused; NCBI BLAST+ deletes them without reading the subject's translation and finishes, and LOSAT now writes the same output.
