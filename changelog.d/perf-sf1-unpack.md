- `blastn -task megablast` writes the one-byte copy of a subject that its preliminary greedy alignment reads into the copy's capacity instead of a zeroed buffer (the copy's every byte is written). Outputs do not change. On the workstation, 200 draft contigs against ref_prok_rep_genomes (27 volumes) at 16 threads: 41.9 → 41.3 s; against one volume 1.88 → 1.84 s at 16 threads and 27.1 → 26.8 s at one thread.
