- `blastn -ungapped` now writes `-outfmt 8`, `9`, `11` and `12`, byte for byte as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does: each ungapped HSP is one alignment record, with the same coordinates, strands and scores, for `-subject` and `-db`, in all four tasks, with `-query_loc`, `-subject_loc` and `-strand`. Before, these four formats were refused with `-ungapped`. `-outfmt 17` (SAM) with `-ungapped` is still refused.
