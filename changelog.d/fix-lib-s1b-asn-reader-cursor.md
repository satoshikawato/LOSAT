- The ASN.1 text reader (rpsbproc's BLAST archive input with its Seq-ids, psiblast and tblastn `-in_pssm`, makeblastdb `-mask_data`) keeps its read position in registers while it reads a value, instead of loading it from memory for every byte: rpsbproc reads a BLAST archive in about 15-17 % less time, makeblastdb with ASN.1 text `-mask_data` runs about 9 % faster, and where the program's stack happens to land now moves rpsbproc's time by about 0.7 % instead of up to about 2.5 %. Output, errors and refusals do not change.
