- BLASTX SEG: when the left part of a trimmed low-complexity segment holds several segments, only the first one found is masked, as in NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 (`blast_seg.c`). This applies to the query's SEG (the default `-seg`, with or without `-lcase_masking`) and to the SEG of the subject in the composition-based statistics redo. Before, BLASTX masked every one of them, so on queries or subjects with such regions the masks (`-outfmt 11`, `-outfmt 16`), the HSPs and the e-values could differ from NCBI's.
