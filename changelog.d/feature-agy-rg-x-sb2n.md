- `blastn` and `blastx` write the results of the earlier query batches before they stop at a later batch's refusal, as NCBI BLAST+ writes them before it fails there: a `CHUNK_SIZE`/`OVERLAP_CHUNK_SIZE` pair that leaves a piece of the batch without a query (and in `blastx` one that NCBI would split again), a negative pair whose pieces leave gaps (`blastn`), an overlap that is not a multiple of 3 (`blastx`), a header record that cannot be read where the search reads it again under `-negative_seqidlist`, and in `blastx` a `-threshold` whose words fill NCBI's overflow banks, which now exits with status 1 as a refusal instead of an engine error. Before, `blastn` wrote nothing of the earlier batches for a negative pair (only the start of the `-outfmt 0` report) and in `-outfmt 5`. The stops of a split batch now come before the database's errors, as in NCBI BLAST+; a batch with no subject to search is reported as NCBI BLAST+ reports it where `blastn` (gaps) and `blastx` (overlap not a multiple of 3) refused it.
