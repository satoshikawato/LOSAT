- `-outfmt 11` (the BLAST archive, `Blast4-archive`) in `tblastn`, with `-subject` and `-db`, as in `blastn`, `blastp` and `blastx`: one archive per query batch (the request with its protein query and nucleotide subject Bioseqs and its algorithm options, the Seq-aligns of outfmt 8, the SEG and lower-case masks of the queries and the `LCaseMask` parameters of `-lcase_masking`, the Karlin-Altschul blocks and search statistics of the batch's first query, then the messages of the batch), the same bytes as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0's `-num_threads 1` run on the fixtures and probes of the item (150 of 205 probe searches and 1,374 of 1,380 randomized `-subject` and `-db` runs, at 1, 2 and 4 threads and with each strict-set switch of the search turned off; of the 55 other probes 53 use what LOSAT's `tblastn` refuses, such as `-task tblastn-fast`, other matrices, word sizes and thresholds, `-ungapped`, `-max_intron_length`, `-comp_based_stats 1` and `3`, a non-integer `BATCH_SIZE` or a title with a byte outside `0x20..=0x7E`, the other two are `-subject` with `-db_gencode` 2 and 32, see below; the six runs are the `-max_target_seqs` with `-num_descriptions` usage error, which NCBI reports with another exit status), an error archive after the archives of the batches before when the run stops with NCBI's own error (a reader error in a later batch, a missing `-db`, `-subject` or `-query` file, a `-subject_loc` or `-query_loc` that NCBI rejects), `ARCHIVE_FORMAT` read as NCBI reads it (`xml` is refused for now) and `BATCH_SIZE` as the batch size of the archives (as for every format). A query or subject title, or a `-db` name, with a byte outside `0x20..=0x7E` (NCBI writes `#`) is refused before the first byte, also in a batch that NCBI completes before a reader error. `tblastn` searches all batches before it writes any format, so the archives are written at the end of the run, one per batch; a run that writes the archive reads the `-subject` records with their letters (the `LOSAT_X_TBNSTORE=0` side of the search), because every archive repeats the whole subjects. `tblastn -subject` with a `-db_gencode` other than 1 differs from NCBI in the search itself (`AGENTS.md`, `PD-TLOSAN-LOCAL-GENCODE-32`), so its archive does too; `-db` with any genetic code is NCBI's. Memory on 400 proteins against the 4.6 Mb *E. coli* K-12 genome (7 query batches, 12.8 MB and 31.8 MB of archive, byte-identical to NCBI's): `-db` 26.5 MiB (outfmt 8: 26.5 MiB; NCBI 82.3 MiB), `-subject` 29.4 MiB (outfmt 8: 26.0 MiB; NCBI 88.8 MiB). Other formats' bytes and memory are unchanged.
