- `tblastn` and `tblastx` read `-db_soft_mask ID` and `-db_hard_mask ID` with `-db`, with the output of NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0: each mask covers the codons of its bases in the six reading frames of the database sequence, words inside a soft mask start no hits, and a hard mask cuts the masked codons out of the search (each frame is searched in pieces between its masks, and the composition-based statistics, the traceback and the re-scoring of tblastx read the masked bases as any base). With `-db_hard_mask`, a database with a sequence whose mask leaves a reading frame without letters after it (the frame's piece from its start runs to its end) is refused before anything is written, where NCBI BLAST+ scans the masked letters past that piece and, in tblastx, writes the same alignment many times (line NP, M4b).
