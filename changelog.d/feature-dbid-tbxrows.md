- tblastx's pairwise report (`-outfmt 0`) makes the displayed alignments of a window of queries at a time and drops them after writing them, as NCBI prints one query's result set at a time and as blastn does, instead of making those of every query of the report first. With 50 of the records of `18S_fungal_sequences` against that database, `-outfmt 0` used 797.7 MiB at 1 thread and 914.2 MiB at 4 (NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0: 427.6 MiB). Reports do not change.
