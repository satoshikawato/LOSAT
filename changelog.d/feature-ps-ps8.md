- `losat psiblast -phi_pattern FILE` runs PHI-BLAST, a search that keeps only the alignments containing a PROSITE pattern found in the query, for one round (`-num_iterations 1`) with `-db` or `-subject`: the pattern's places in the query, their E-values from NCBI's pattern statistics, the reports of `-outfmt 0` (a table and the alignments for each place, with a `pattern` line), `6`, `7`, `10` and `20`, the queries' messages, the pattern errors, the error of `-outfmt 8`, `9` and `12`, and the report of a matrix and gap costs without NCBI's pattern statistics (its message and no hits) are NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0's. Refused by name: rounds after the first, `-outfmt` 1-5, 11, 13-16 and 18, `-sum_stats true`, a subject longer than 5,000,000 residues, `phi_pattern` in the library and pylosat, and the patterns and hit counts that take NCBI past its arrays.
