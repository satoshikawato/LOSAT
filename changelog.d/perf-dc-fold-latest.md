- `blastn -task dc-megablast` finds its first hits faster on long queries: the two-hit step keeps only the latest hit of each diagonal in a compact table of its own instead of walking chains of older entries, and the threads no longer copy that table at every step (dmel cDNAs, 31,168 queries, against 3 Mb of the dmel genome: x0.846 of the time at one thread, x0.912 at 8 and x0.917 at 16 threads, peak memory +0.5 % or less) (`LOSAT_X_DCLATEST`, a switch of the strict set, so reports do not change; `=0` turns it off) ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
