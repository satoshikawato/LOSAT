- `blastp` and `blastx` read `-db_soft_mask ID` and `-db_hard_mask ID` with `-db`, with the output of NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0: words inside a soft mask start no hits, a hard mask cuts the masked letters out of the search (the database sequences are searched in pieces between their masks, and the composition-based statistics and the traceback score the masked letters as `X`), and the alignments of outfmt 0 to 4 show the masked letters in lower case. With `-db_hard_mask`, a database with a sequence whose first mask runs to its end (a masked tail), or one with more than 5,000,000 letters between two masks, is refused before anything is written, where NCBI BLAST+ reads past those letters or searches in overlapping parts (line NP, M4b).
