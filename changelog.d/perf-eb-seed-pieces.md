- `blastn` with `-subject` (dc-megablast, blastn, megablast and blastn-short at two threads or more) holds less memory in its seed stage: a strip's seeds now wait for the partitions' folds in pieces of a fixed size, the scan stopping where a piece is full and going on from there, instead of in lists that kept the largest capacity any strip had needed. Reports do not change. Sakai x EDL933 (5.5 Mb genomes) dc-megablast: peak memory 0.77 of the previous version's at 4 threads, 0.85 at 16 (166 -> 142 MiB, NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0: 164) and 0.72 at 32; blastn 0.95 at 16 and 0.90 at 32 threads; one thread unchanged. The stage now holds at most (3 x threads + 2) pieces of 16,384 seeds whatever the subject or the query (`LOSAT_X_SEEDPIECE`, a switch of the strict set: `=0` runs the previous form) ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
