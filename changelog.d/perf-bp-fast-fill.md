- `blastp -task blastp-fast` fills its compressed lookup table by buckets of cells at one thread (`LOSAT_X_BPFFILL`, a new switch of the strict set; natively and in WebAssembly): the table, the reports and max RSS do not change. One thread: the human proteome x the yeast proteome (OrthoFinder, `-db`) takes 0.94 of the time (68.6 -> 64.2 s), the Arabidopsis proteome x the zebrafish proteome 0.99 (233.2 -> 230.1 s), 1/10 of the human proteins x the yeast proteome 0.87 of the cycles.
