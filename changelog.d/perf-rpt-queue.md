- `tblastx` with two or more threads computes the report's per-HSP values (the displayed identities and the outfmt 0 rows, each HSP's codons translated again) and formats the tabular rows on the search's threads after the last query batch instead of on one thread while the others wait (`LOSAT_X_TBXRPTPAR`, one of the switches above from this change; `=0` or `LOSAT_X_DEFAULT=0` turns it off; `LOSAT_X_TBXRPTPARSHADOW=1` also runs the one-thread stage and compares). Reports, `-out` files and standard error do not change. That stage took 1.31 s (outfmt 6) and 3.37 s (outfmt 0) of a three-genome search at 16 threads on the workstation ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
