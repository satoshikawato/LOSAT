- `blastx` reads `-query_loc` and `-subject_loc` and gives the output of NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 for outfmt 0, 6, 7, 10 and 20 (with custom fields), with `-subject` and, for `-query_loc`, `-db`: each record is cut to its interval before translation, the query batches count whole records as NCBI's reader does, a query record whose interval starts more than one letter past its end is not searched (a batch of such records alone ends the run with NCBI's `Empty CBlastQueryVector`), and the reports show record coordinates and frames, the record's `Length=` and `slen`, `qlen` and `qcovs` of the range as given, and in outfmt 0 the masks of the frame NCBI labels them with. The other formats with a range are refused until a later change. A value that is not UTF-8 in any `blastx` option now gets the explicit rejection of the other programs instead of the argument parser's error. Searches without a range do not change.
