- `tblastn` and `tblastx` read `-db_soft_mask ID` and `-db_hard_mask ID` with `-db`, with the output of NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0: each mask covers the codons of its bases in the six reading frames of the database sequence, words inside a soft mask start no hits, and a hard mask cuts the masked codons out of the search (each frame is searched in pieces between its masks, and the composition-based statistics, the traceback and the re-scoring of tblastx read the masked bases as any base). With `-db_hard_mask`, a database with a sequence whose masks leave NCBI BLAST+ a part of a reading frame, from its first letter, that NCBI searches as the whole frame while the part stops before the frame's end or follows an empty part (masks at or near either end of a sequence) is refused before anything is written, where NCBI BLAST+ scans the masked letters past that part (in tblastx it writes the same alignment many times) or crashes (line NP, M4b).
