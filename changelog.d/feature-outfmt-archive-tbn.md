- `-outfmt 11` (the BLAST archive, `Blast4-archive`) in `tblastn`, with `-subject` and `-db`, as in `blastn`, `blastp` and `blastx`: one archive per query batch (the request with its protein query and nucleotide subject Bioseqs and its algorithm options, the Seq-aligns of outfmt 8, the SEG and lower-case masks of the queries and the `LCaseMask` parameters of `-lcase_masking`, the Karlin-Altschul blocks and search statistics of the batch's first query, then the messages of the batch), the same bytes as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0's `-num_threads 1` run on the fixtures and probes of the item (150 of the 205 probe searches that LOSAT's `tblastn` accepts and 898 randomized `-subject` and `-db` runs, at 1, 2 and 4 threads and with each strict-set switch of the search turned off; the others are options LOSAT's `tblastn` refuses, such as `-task tblastn-fast`, other matrices and word sizes, `-ungapped` and `-max_intron_length`), an error archive after the archives of the batches before when the run stops with NCBI's own error (a reader error in a later batch, a missing `-db`, `-subject` or `-query` file, a `-subject_loc` or `-query_loc` that NCBI rejects), `ARCHIVE_FORMAT` read as NCBI reads it (`xml` is refused for now) and `BATCH_SIZE` as the batch size of the archives (as for every format). A query or subject title, or a `-db` name, with a byte outside `0x20..=0x7E` (NCBI writes `#`) is refused before the first byte, also in a batch that NCBI completes before a reader error. `tblastn` searches all batches before it writes any format, so the archives are written at the end of the run, one per batch; a run that writes the archive reads the `-subject` records with their letters (the `LOSAT_X_TBNSTORE=0` side of the search), because every archive repeats the whole subjects. `tblastn -subject` with a `-db_gencode` other than 1 differs from NCBI in the search itself (`AGENTS.md`, `PD-TLOSAN-LOCAL-GENCODE-32`), so its archive does too; `-db` with any genetic code is NCBI's. Memory on 400 proteins against the 4.6 Mb *E. coli* K-12 genome (7 query batches, 12.8 MB and 31.8 MB of archive, byte-identical to NCBI's): `-db` 26.3 MiB (outfmt 8: 26.1 MiB; NCBI 81.7 MiB), `-subject` 29.3 MiB (outfmt 8: 25.8 MiB; NCBI 88.9 MiB). Other formats' bytes and memory are unchanged.
