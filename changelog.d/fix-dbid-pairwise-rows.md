- blastn's pairwise report (`-outfmt 0`) makes the displayed alignments of a window of queries at a time and drops them after writing them, as NCBI prints one query's result set at a time, instead of making those of every query of the report first. On a database with parsed Seq-ids (`makeblastdb -parse_seqids`, every NCBI-distributed database), whose report comes after the whole search, megablast of 100 16S sequences against `16S_ribosomal_RNA` used more memory than NCBI BLAST+ (313.7 MiB at 1 thread, 334.2 MiB at 4, NCBI 298.2 MiB). Reports do not change.
