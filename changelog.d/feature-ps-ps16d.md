- `losat rpsbproc` reads the BlastOutput XML of `rpsblast -outfmt 5` and `rpstblastn -outfmt 5`, which NCBI's rpsbproc 0.5.1 reads as deprecated (`Warning: XML format support is deprecated!`), and writes what NCBI writes, byte for byte: one session per `BlastOutput` document, read one after another from the input until one does not read (a truncated or malformed document, trailing text, an ASN.1 archive, BLAST XML2), with the program's version, the database, the matrix and the E-value threshold of the document on the `SESSION` line; per `Iteration`, the query's label, title and length from the Iteration, the HSPs whose E-value passes `-e` (unlike an archive, the cutoff drops hits; a NaN E-value passes), the PSSM-ID from `Hit_accession` as C's `atoi` reads it (`lcl` ids give 0), the segments and the query's residues from the alignment strings (`ParseAlignStrings`: `-` where no alignment covers the query), the reading frame from `Hsp_query-frame`, so that a query without kept hits prints as `Peptide` and a nucleotide query's sites read the residues of its alignments, not a translation; NCBI's XML reader (`CObjectIStreamXml`: declaration, DOCTYPE, comments and processing instructions, entity and character references, CDATA, the declared encoding, CRLF, members in the module's order, OPTIONAL members, self-closed elements, NCBI's number syntax); and NCBI's exceptions (exit 3): a missing `Parameters_matrix`, `Hsp_align-len`, `Hsp_identity` or `Hsp_query-frame` (`CUnassignedMember`, the session line cut before the matrix), and libstdc++'s `basic_string::replace` out of range for an alignment outside its query. Memory: per document LOSAT keeps the queries' hits and residues, not the document's text. Refused (PD-LOSAT-NCBI-DEFECTS 1.17; none occurs in NCBI's writers' output): XML attributes, namespace prefixes and control characters in character data, an `Hsp_hseq` shorter than its `Hsp_qseq` by more than one letter, an alignment that writes a residue other than NUL at or past the end of its query's frame string, a query whose strings NCBI holds as 1 GiB or more (a nucleotide query of 0 or 1 base among them), and coordinate arithmetic of an HSP that overflows NCBI's `int` (a coordinate of `-2147483648`, a range length, domain positions in the alignment strings, an `Hsp_qseq` of 2^31 letters). Also for archives: a NaN read from the text (`nan`, `NOT-A-NUMBER`) is NCBI's `HUGE_VAL/HUGE_VAL`, printed `-nan`; hits with NaN E-values or bit scores are kept and sorted as NCBI's `std::sort` sorts them (PS16a refused every NaN), refused only where that sort would read outside its vector.
