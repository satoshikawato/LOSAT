- `tblastn`: the offset-pair array of the lookup scan (one per running unit) holds NCBI's 4,096 + the longest lookup chain instead of 32,768 + it where the scan runs on presence bytes (AVX2 CPUs; sparse tables on x86-64 without AVX2 and in WebAssembly): a scan call that stops on a full array keeps its block of words, and the next call copies on from the word that did not fit; reports do not change, and `LOSAT_X_TBNSCANRESUME=0` restores the former size ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
