- `tblastn` with four or more query batches keeps the subjects' six-frame translations for the later batches only when they hold no more than its threads could have in flight (the thread count times the translation of the longest subject): one genome or a few chromosomes are kept, many subjects are translated again for each batch and dropped after the subject, as NCBI BLAST+ does (`LOSAT_X_TBNKEEPFEW`, one of the switches above; `=0` turns it off; shadow `LOSAT_X_TBNKEEPFEWSHADOW`). Reports do not change. 118 fly proteins (five batches) against 57,775 zebrafish cDNAs (`-db`): peak anonymous memory 361 -> 52 MiB at one thread and 390 -> 81 MiB at 8 (NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 16 and 28 MiB), 0.98-0.99 of the time at 8 and 16 threads; against 28,036 16S records 134 -> 19 MiB and 152 -> 38 MiB, 0.98-0.99 of the time at 4 to 16 threads ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
