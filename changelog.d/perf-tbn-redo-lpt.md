- `tblastn` with composition-based statistics (the default) on 2 or more threads: the matches whose alignments are redone are shared out among the threads one at a time, those with the most HSPs first, instead of in runs of adjacent matches, where one thread could get all the heavy matches of one protein. Reports do not change; `LOSAT_X_TBNREDOLPT=0` restores the previous sharing. 122 human proteins against the human genome (`-db`) at 8 threads: 1,880 → 1,219 s; 35 of them at 16 threads 397 → 284 s; on later code, at 24 threads 53.3 → 33.2 s and at 32 threads 36.1 → 29.7 s ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
