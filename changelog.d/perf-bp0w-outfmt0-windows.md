- `losat blastp`, `losat psiblast` and `losat rpsblast` use less memory for reports that show the alignments: outfmt 0, BLAST XML (outfmt 5, 13 to 16) and tabular reports with `qseq`, `sseq`, `btop` or other fields that read the aligned letters now hold the alignment rows of a few queries at a time instead of those of a whole query block, as `losat blastn` and `losat tblastx` already do. The output is unchanged.
