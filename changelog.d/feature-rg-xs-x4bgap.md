- `losat blastp`, `blastx` and `tblastn -use_sw_tback`: a Smith-Waterman alignment that starts with a gap in the query's row is reported as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 reports it, byte for byte and with its exit status. The tables (`length`, `gaps`, `gapopen`, `pident`, `ppos`, also in the default fields of outfmt 6 and 7) and the `Gaps =` of outfmt 0 count only the columns inside the query's aligned range (a 285-column alignment that starts with 95 gaps: length 190, gaps 0, 100 % identity), and BLAST XML, JSON and XML2 show the end gaps of a row as spaces. With `-subject`, BLASTX and TBLASTN leave out the first segment of the alignment where it has a gap, as NCBI's mapping of the subject's location does: the alignment starts at the first aligned letter (`Identities = 190/190`) in every format. With `-db`, TBLASTN stops where NCBI cannot take the range of such a query row, as BLASTX already did: outfmt 0 to 4 at the description table, `-sorthsps 2`, `qcovs`, outfmt 13 and 14 and `-html` (exit status 255), BLAST XML, JSON and XML2 with the exception in their document (exit status 0); the tables without `qcovs`, the Seq-aligns and outfmt 0 to 4 without the description table are written. BLASTX's query-anchored views (outfmt 1 to 4) put such an alignment under NCBI's frame, the minus frame that the query's length gives, and outfmt 0 counts the positions of such an alignment of a minus-strand query down, as NCBI does (line RG, item X4b-gap).
