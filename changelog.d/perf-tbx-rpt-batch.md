- `tblastx` with two or more query batches puts each batch's HSPs in report order when the batch has been searched, while the next batch is searched, instead of ordering every batch's HSPs on one thread after the last batch (`LOSAT_X_TBXRPTBATCH`, one of the switches of the strict set, so reports do not change; `LOSAT_X_TBXRPTBATCH=0` turns it off). A `-db` search orders each batch once instead of twice (it ordered a copy for the titles of the reported sequences). On the workstation three ~650 kb genomes against three (genetic code 4, three batches) took x0.995 of the time at 16 threads; the same queries against the three genomes cut into 18,838 pieces of 100 bp x0.992 with a peak memory of 210 MiB (299 MiB before), against one genome x0.990 with 247 MiB (266 MiB); Sakai against MG1655 at 16 threads peaks at 1,962 MiB (2,157 MiB). A search of one query batch orders once at its end, as before.
