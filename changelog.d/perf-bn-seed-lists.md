- `blastn -task blastn-short` runs about 3-4 % faster at 8 and 16 threads on query sets above 8,000 letters, `-task blastn` about 1 % faster, and both hold less memory: the seed stage split over the threads now hands each part its seeds as it scans, as dc-megablast's stage does (236 D. melanogaster cDNAs against 3 Mb of chromosome 2L, blastn-short at 16 threads: 2.36 -> 2.29 s, max RSS 408 -> 385 MiB) (`LOSAT_X_BNSEEDLISTS`, a switch of the strict set, so reports do not change; `=0` turns it off) ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
