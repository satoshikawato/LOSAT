- `blastn -task dc-megablast` runs about 5 % faster at 8 and 16 threads on large query sets and holds less memory: the lookup table built on the thread pool is built in steps over two bucket sets with one barrier per step, each strip's seeds go straight to the list of their partition, and the partitions' folds no longer record the diagonal buckets they change (31,168 D. melanogaster cDNAs against 3 Mb of chromosome 2L at 16 threads: 1.42 -> 1.35 s, max RSS 235 -> 220 MiB) (`LOSAT_X_DCSEEDLEAN`, a switch of the strict set, so reports do not change; `=0` turns it off) ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
