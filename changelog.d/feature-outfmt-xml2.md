- `-outfmt 15` (single-file BLAST JSON) and `-outfmt 16` (single-file BLAST XML2) in `blastp`, with `-subject` and `-db`: the `CBlastOutput2` documents that NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 writes, byte for byte at every thread count (one report per query, the `Subject_N` list, the query masks, lower-case masked letters, the `-db` taxid, the one-query delay of a `-subject` search). A byte outside US-ASCII in a title or in the `-db` name, and a `-db` taxid with `taxdb.bti` on the search path, which LOSAT cannot write as NCBI does, are refused. Outfmt 0/5/6/7 are unchanged.
