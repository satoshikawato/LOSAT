- `losat rpsblast` takes `-use_sw_tback`: with composition-based statistics (`-comp_based_stats` 1, the default, 2 and 3) it reports NCBI's Smith-Waterman alignments of each profile, as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does, byte for byte (apart from the version line, which names LOSAT), at every thread count; without composition-based statistics it changes nothing, as in NCBI. A profile whose Smith-Waterman redo NCBI repeats without end (the e-value of a score of 0 passes `-evalue`, as with a very large `-evalue` or a very small `-searchsp`, and the profile's first position scores the query's first letter above 0) is refused at once; the other profiles of that regime are dropped at once, as NCBI drops them after its rounds. Alignments that start or end with a gap in one sequence are shown as NCBI shows them, with spaces for that sequence's end gaps in the pairwise report and in the tabular `qseq`, `sseq` and `btop`. `blastp`'s and `psiblast`'s `-use_sw_tback` stay refused (line PS, series PR, item PS11c-3; DECISIONS 26 D1 and D7, 63).
