- `blastn -db NAME -db_hard_mask ID` searches with the database's masks of the filtering algorithm `ID` cut out of the search, with the output of NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 in every format (apart from the version line, which names LOSAT): the database sequences are searched in pieces between their masks, an alignment that crosses a mask in the traceback scores the masked letters as `B`, and the identities of the displayed rows (outfmt 0, 5, 13 to 16, tabular rows with sequence columns) count the stored letters while the Seq-align's (outfmt 8, 9, 11, 12, the standard tabular columns, SAM) count the masked ones as mismatches. A database with a searched sequence of at least the word's letters whose first mask with letters runs to its end with at most 5,000,000 letters and no other mask before it than masks of no letters at its start (all masked, or a masked tail), whose masks are out of order, or whose last mask is shorter than the letters before its end in its last piece where that piece and an earlier one that ends after its start both hold a word (and the stretch after the mask is one piece) is refused before anything is written, where NCBI BLAST+ reads past its buffers (line NP, M4a-2b).
