- `blastx -task blastx-fast` and `blastx -word_size 5` build their compressed lookup table flat, as `blastp-fast` and TBLASTN do (`LOSAT_X_BPCLUT`, on unless `=0`): each query unit's table no longer holds about 29 MiB for its fixed cells. Reports do not change.
- `blastx -task blastx-fast` and `blastx -word_size 5` search at most 3 query units at a time, or `ceil(threads / subjects)` with few subjects (`LOSAT_X_BXCLUTCAP[=K]`, on unless `=0`). With the flat table, Trinity x Swiss-Prot at 8 threads holds 70 MiB of anonymous memory instead of 231 MiB, in 0.86 of the time. Reports do not change.
