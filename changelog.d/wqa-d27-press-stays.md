- LOSAT Web: a press of a button, a tab or another control is a click on it also when what the page shows above it
  changes between the press and the release: an input card's warning that comes as its check ends, the cards while
  the inputs are read and checked again after `-parse_deflines` is typed, a line that comes or goes above the control.
  From a press until its click, while the pointer is on the pressed control, the page keeps the control where it was
  under the pointer, and at least as large (the label "Run LOSAT (2 runs)" loses its count while the inputs are read).
  Before, the control could move from under the pointer by 35 to 175 px, and the release then did nothing and said
  nothing: Run LOSAT started no run.
  A press with the keys, which goes to the focused control wherever it is, is not changed.
