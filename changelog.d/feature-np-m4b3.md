- `blastp`, `blastx`, `tblastn` and `tblastx` with `-db_hard_mask ID` search a database sequence whose first mask runs to its end (a masked tail or a sequence masked entirely; in `blastp` and `blastx` also after empty masks at its start), which they refused before: the part before the mask is searched alone, as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 searches it in the same database with the mask shortened until a letter follows that part (one letter in `blastp` and `blastx`; in `tblastn` and `tblastx` per reading frame, a few bases, for masks at or near either end of a sequence). The masked letters stay masked in the alignments and the statistics. NCBI itself scans the masked letters past that part there, and crashes, reports other alignments, or in `tblastx` writes one alignment many times (an approved NCBI defect, PD-LOSAT-NCBI-DEFECTS exception 20; line NP, M4b-3).
