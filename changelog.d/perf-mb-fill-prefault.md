- `blastn` megablast and blastn with two or more threads write one zero to each page of the contiguous megablast lookup table that the read-once fill is about to update, before its first update (`LOSAT_X_MBLUTPREFAULT`, one of the switches above since 2026-10-19; `=0` or `LOSAT_X_DEFAULT=0` turns it off), so that Linux maps those pages once instead of first to its zero page and then by copy-on-write, which flushed each page from the TLBs of every other CPU of the search. Only pages the fill writes are touched, each element keeps its value, and the table and reports do not change. On an i9 (WSL2, alternating pairs) megablast of three E. coli genomes against LC738868 took x0.61-0.69 of the time at 4-32 threads, MG1655 x0.88-0.93 at 16-32 threads and 31,168 D. melanogaster cDNAs against 3 Mb of chromosome 2L x0.78-0.79 at 8-32 (x0.86-0.92 without `LOSAT_X_QBATCHPAR`'s slots); max RSS did not change where one fill runs at a time, and moved within the slots' admitted memory (-26 to +21 MiB) where slots fill tables side by side ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
