- Under a negative seqid list, a database sequence whose definition lines NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 cannot read (a damaged record, or a `gnl|BL_ORD_ID|` line with a text tag in a later volume) no longer makes BLASTP, PSIBLAST, BLASTX or TBLASTN refuse a search that NCBI completes: as in NCBI, the composition-based or Smith-Waterman redo skips such a weak match once the hit list is full, and LOSAT writes NCBI's output; it refuses only where NCBI's redo or traceback reads that sequence and stops (line RG, D1c-1c).
