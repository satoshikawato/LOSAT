- On a damaged taxonomy database whose last entry begins past the end of `taxdb.btd` and whose volume also holds another tax id of that entry's slot of NCBI BLAST+'s 256-slot taxonomy cache, LOSAT now tracks NCBI BLAST+'s per-batch scope lifetime (`ResetScopeHistory`) and BLAST XML (`-outfmt 5`) scope retention across all search programs (and across rounds of one query in `psiblast`). Another sequence id previously held by a Bioseq in an earlier batch now loads fresh after a batch scope reset instead of being refused, and multi-batch `-outfmt 5` searches keep prefetched sequences pinned across batches (and non-prefetched sequences up to 20 sequences) without over-refusing.
