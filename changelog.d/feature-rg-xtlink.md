- `losat tblastx` and `losat tblastn -ungapped` with a very short query against one very short subject (for example 11 bases against 12 to 14 bases, or 6 to 16 bases against 8 to 20 bases with `-word_size 2`), where NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 crashes in its sum-statistics linking: LOSAT links with NCBI's cutoff and writes the hits that NCBI writes for the nearest search it completes (a second subject of `N` and `-dbsize` of the subject's length). Where the expected length of the linking cutoffs is negative, LOSAT now takes NCBI's cutoff (it used 1); the rows of the tested searches do not change.
