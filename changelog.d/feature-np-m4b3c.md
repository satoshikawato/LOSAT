- `tblastn` and `tblastx` with `-db_hard_mask ID` search a database sequence with a mask within its first two bases, or within the one or two bases after its last whole codon, or with an empty mask at its start, which they refused before: such a mask leaves a reading frame an empty part at its start, after which NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 searches the rest of the frame whole and crashes in every run. LOSAT searches that frame as outside the mask, the masked bases staying masked in every frame, as NCBI searches the same database with that short mask removed and its bases stored as `N` (approved exception 27 of the decision record on NCBI BLAST+ defects).
