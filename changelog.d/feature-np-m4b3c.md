- `tblastn` and `tblastx` with `-db_hard_mask ID` search a database sequence with a mask within its first two bases, or within the one or two bases after its last whole codon, or with an empty mask at its start, which they refused before: such a mask leaves a reading frame an empty part at its start, after which NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 searches the rest of the frame whole and crashes in every run. LOSAT searches that frame as a frame without a mask, the 1 or 2 masked bases staying masked in every frame (approved exception 27 of the decision record on NCBI BLAST+ defects). In the cases checked, the rows are those of NCBI's run on the same database with the short mask dropped and its bases stored as `N`, and of NCBI's own runs where another mask of the sequence avoids the crash; NCBI's preliminary search reads a stored `N` as a random base where LOSAT reads the real one, so a result can differ from that run.
