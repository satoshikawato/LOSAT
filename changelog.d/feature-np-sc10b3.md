- `tblastx -word_size 4`, with every `-matrix`: the search takes the unit path of word sizes 2 and 3 (the seed stage of one subject split over the threads by subject frame, chunk and context group) instead of the reference frame loop. The lookup tables of the query-context groups are built from the query by a new builder with 32-bit cell indices that counts, lays out and fills them on the search's threads, without NCBI's chains; output unchanged at 1, 4 and 8 threads. On a pair of 0.6 Mb genomes, word size 4 takes 0.33 of its previous time at 1 thread and 0.10 at 8 threads with BLOSUM62 (0.37 and 0.07 with BLOSUM45), and its peak memory falls from 4,264 to 2,164 MiB with BLOSUM62 (NCBI 4,129) and from 5,685 to 2,868 MiB with BLOSUM45 (NCBI 5,363); 50 cDNAs against one genome take 0.32 and 0.14 of their previous time. The groups of 4-letter words are never cut into query slices (each slice would repeat a table larger than its window). The default search (BLOSUM62, word size 3) runs the same machine code.
