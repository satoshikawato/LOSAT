- `blastn -task dc-megablast` spends less time in the two-hit seed fold: a retrieve from the diagonal hash is answered from a per-bucket memo of the latest insert when every other cell of the bucket is too old to matter (`LOSAT_X_DIAGLV2`), and the strips folds skip a call that always returns at once for a discontiguous word and inline the hash insert (`LOSAT_X_FOLDLEAN`). Both are in the default set; `=0` turns either off, `LOSAT_X_DIAGLVSHADOW=1` checks every memo answer. On the workstation 31,168 Drosophila cDNAs against 3 Mb of its genome ran in 0.88 of the time at 16 and 8 threads (unchanged at 1 thread) and 1,775 cDNAs in 0.89 at 16 threads, with no more memory. Reports do not change. ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md))
