- `blastn -task dc-megablast` finds its seeds faster on large query sets: the scan of a word's query offsets reads the context index's form, the word length and the subject's bound once per strip or word instead of once per offset, and adds each word's seeds to its list at once (31,168 dmel cDNAs against 3 Mb of the dmel genome: 10.781 to 10.181 s at 1 thread, x0.94; 1.894 to 1.863 s at 8 threads, x0.98; 1.130 to 1.122 s at 16 threads, x0.99, cycles x0.98) (`LOSAT_X_DCEMITHOIST`, a switch of the strict set, so reports do not change; `=0` turns it off) ([`docs/agents/diagnostics.md`](docs/agents/diagnostics.md)).
