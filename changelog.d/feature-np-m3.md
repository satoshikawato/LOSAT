- `[WINDOW_MASKER] WINDOW_MASKER_PATH` in `.ncbirc` and `NCBI_CONFIG__WINDOW_MASKER__WINDOW_MASKER_PATH` are accepted (every program; they change no output without blastn `-window_masker_taxid`). LOSAT reads the directory of `-window_masker_taxid` as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does: `NCBI_CONFIG__WINDOW_MASKER__WINDOW_MASKER_PATH`, then `WINDOW_MASKER_PATH`, then the first `.ncbirc` (also when the program's `.ini` turns `.ncbirc` off), then the current directory, and finds `<directory>/<taxid>/wmasker.obinary`.
