- megablast: the test of whether a query offset is in a lookup word's chain (the seed-mask check of each word) stops at the first match, as NCBI's `s_MBLookup` does, instead of walking the whole chain. Against a database of 1,000,000 short sequences whose query words hit long chains, one thread took 48.0 s instead of 63.9 s on the workstation (NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0: 50.6 s); other searches take the same time or less. Reports do not change.
