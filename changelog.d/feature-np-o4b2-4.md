- `losat blastp -ungapped` with `-negative_seqidlist`: a database sequence that the list keeps while removing all its definition lines now keeps its place until the hit list is cut, as in NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0, and is then left out of the reports. Before, it was left out at the start, so with `-culling_limit` such a run could report more alignments than NCBI BLAST+ (for example a second subject under `-culling_limit 2`) or warn about a header that NCBI BLAST+ never reads, and a database sequence whose header cannot be read stopped the search even when it was not reported.
