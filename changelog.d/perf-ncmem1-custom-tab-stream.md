- `blastn` with `LOSAT_X_RPTSTREAM` (on by default) now also writes the tabular reports with a field list or a delimiter and outfmt 10 and 20 batch by batch, as it writes the default outfmt 6 and 7, instead of keeping every batch's final hit lists until one write after the search (`off=custom_tabular` is gone; SISTR's cgMLST search: max RSS 340 -> 260 MiB). It no longer reads every sequence of a `-db` (definition lines, ambiguity data, taxonomy) or every subject's id before the search (mlst's 225,777 sequences: 0.63 s): as NCBI does, each batch checks the sequences it reports. Reports, `-out` files, standard error and exit status do not change for inputs that LOSAT does not refuse. Where LOSAT refuses a reported sequence that only a later batch reports (a definition line, parsed Seq-id, ambiguity data or taxonomy lookup that it cannot write as NCBI, a `Subject_` id), the refusal now comes after the reports and query warnings of the batches before, with the same exit status, instead of before every report (where several sequences are refused, the one named is the first of that batch, not the first of the whole search); an error of the taxonomy database or of reading the database that a later batch meets likewise follows the reports of the batches before, in NCBI's order (the `off=` reasons `subject_ids`, `titles`, `joined`, `large_parsed`, `ambiguity` and `taxonomy` are gone). Outfmt 20's line of the field names comes with the first batch's report.
