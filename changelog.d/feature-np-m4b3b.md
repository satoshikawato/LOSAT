- `blastp` and `blastx` with `-db_hard_mask ID` now search a database whose masks overlap, nest or are stored out of order, which they refused before, with NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0's rows byte for byte, also where a part between overlapping masks is shorter than the word. `segmasker` writes such masks. A database is still refused when one of its sequences has one of these shapes, and the error names the sequence: overlapping masks after which NCBI's word hits reach the table of diagonals of the sequences searched before or after it (its rows then vary from run to run with threads); a part shorter than the word whose seed NCBI's gapped search never ends on, or whose alignments NCBI merges with another part's (found during the search); masks out of order whose parts NCBI merges across an overlap; a mask that runs to the end with masks stored after it; and a mask that ends before it starts. With `segmasker`'s defaults, A. thaliana TAIR10 and the UniRef50 sequences we measured have sequences of the first shape, so those databases are still refused. `tblastn` and `tblastx` still refuse overlapping or out-of-order masks (line NP, M4b-3b).
