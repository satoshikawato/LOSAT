- `losat tblastn -ungapped -comp_based_stats 0` searches without gaps, as NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 does, with `-subject` and `-db` and in every output format: the alignments are the ungapped extensions of the word hits, scored again on the subject's letters, linked by sum statistics (or given e-values without it) with the ungapped Karlin-Altschul values, and the reports show what NCBI's show for an ungapped search (the linked set's size in the pairwise descriptions, no gapped statistics, no gap costs). `-subject_besthit` and the other filters work with it. With `-ungapped`, a `-matrix` name that NCBI BLAST+ does not have gives NCBI's error for each query of the first query batch (exit status 3). `-ungapped` is still refused with `-in_pssm`, and so are a `-matrix` name that begins with `IDENTITY` but is not `IDENTITY` and a matrix file that NCBI BLAST+ would read (NCBI crashes or aborts on all of these); a subject of fewer than 12 bases whose alignment reaches the ungapped re-evaluation is refused as with the gapped search's traceback. `-ungapped` with `-use_sw_tback` is refused at the first query batch that is searched, after the reports of the batches before, as in `blastx`: NCBI BLAST+ crashes at every query batch it searches with them.
- `losat tblastn`, gapped searches too: the identities and positives of the reports (tabular `pident`, `nident`, `mismatch`, `positive`, the pairwise `Identities` and `Positives`, XML `Hsp_identity` and `Hsp_positive`) are counted on the letters the reports show, as NCBI does. A subject codon that can be read as D or N, E or Q, or I or L (shown as B, Z or J) now counts as NCBI counts it against the query: before, a query X against such a codon was counted as an identity and a query B or Z as a mismatch. Scores, e-values, coordinates and the Seq-aligns do not change.
- `losat blastp`, `psiblast`, `rpsblast` and `tblastn`: an `-evalue` of exactly 1.7976931348623157e308 now runs without composition-based statistics (`-comp_based_stats 0`, `-matrix IDENTITY`, `tblastn -ungapped`), where NCBI BLAST+ 2.17.0 runs it too. It is still refused with composition-based statistics 1 to 3, where NCBI crashes on some inputs, and an infinite `-evalue` is still refused in every mode.
