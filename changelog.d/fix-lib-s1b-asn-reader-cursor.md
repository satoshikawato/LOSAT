- The ASN.1 text reader (rpsbproc's BLAST archive input with its Seq-ids, psiblast and tblastn `-in_pssm`, makeblastdb `-mask_data`) keeps its read position in registers while it reads a value, instead of loading it from memory for every byte: rpsbproc reads a BLAST archive in about 15-17 % less time, and makeblastdb with ASN.1 text `-mask_data` takes about 9 % less time. Where the program's stack happens to land now moves rpsbproc's time less (over 256 placements, single runs 1.5 % apart instead of 2.8 %). Output, errors and refusals do not change.
