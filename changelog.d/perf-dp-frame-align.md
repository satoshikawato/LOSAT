- The AVX2 X-drop DP kernels (`align8`, `align16`) and the exact Newton solver of composition-based statistics realign their stack frames to 64 bytes, so that their 256-bit register spills never cross a cache line. Before, whether they did depended on the callers' stack depth and, at one thread, on the stack offset that address-space randomisation sets per run, so a change elsewhere in the call chain could move blastp, tblastn or blastn by ±2 % (blastp WSSV × PajaWSV at 8 threads read 1.023 of the cycles on one branch that never touched the DP). On the workstation against the previous dev: blastp WSSV × PajaWSV at 8 threads 0.996 of the cycles, blastx AP027131 × AP027133 0.999, blastn -task blastn (31,168 fly cDNAs × 3 Mb) at 16 threads 0.999, blastp 25 UniRef50 queries × `-db` at 16 threads 1.000, dc-megablast Sakai × EDL933 at one thread 1.003 (0.6 % more instructions: the realigned frame keeps one general register less). Reports do not change.
