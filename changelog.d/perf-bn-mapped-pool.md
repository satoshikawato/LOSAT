- `blastn` at 8 or 16 threads keeps less memory with long database sequences: the large per-subject buffers (database blocks and sequences, their letters, the ungapped hits and their sort's scratch) are returned to the system or handed to the next thread instead of staying in each thread's memory arena. 1,500 D. melanogaster cDNAs against the D. melanogaster genome `-db` at 16 threads: max RSS 624 -> 321 MiB (NCBI 372 MiB), at 8 threads 343 -> 272 MiB (NCBI 336); dc-megablast at 8 threads 222 -> 186 MiB (NCBI 193); same time (`LOSAT_X_BNMAPPED`, one of the switches above; `=0` turns it off; same results).
